Crop Growth Stages & Advisory

Package of practices & critical timings

Wheat (Rabi Season)

Ideal: 15°C to 24°C
Sowing Window: November 1st to November 25th (Timely Sown)
CRI (Crown Root Initiation) (20-25 DAS)

1st irrigation mandatory; top-dress 1/3rd Urea.

Tillering (40-45 DAS)

2nd irrigation; weed eradication using 2,4-D or Clodinafop.

Jointing / Stem Extension (60-65 DAS)

3rd irrigation; watch out for yellow rust symptoms.

Flowering / Heading (80-85 DAS)

4th irrigation; spray 0.5% Potassium Nitrate if moisture stress occurs.

Milking & Grain Filling (100-105 DAS)

Light irrigation on non-windy day to prevent lodging.

Maturity & Harvest (125-135 DAS)

Harvest when grain moisture drops below 12% and straw turns golden brown.

Nutrient Plan: NPK 120:60:40 kg/ha + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate basal.
Pest & Disease Alert: Scout for Yellow/Stripe rust during foggy winter mornings; apply Propiconazole 25% EC at first appearance.
